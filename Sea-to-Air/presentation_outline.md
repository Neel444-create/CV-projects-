# 10-Slide Consulting Presentation Outline

## 1. Executive Summary
Decision question, scope, three routes assessed, headline finding and recommended next step. Populate only after replacing illustrative assumptions with evidence.

## 2. Customer Problem
Explain why selected customers need time-sensitive cargo moved over water. Separate verified customer pain points from hypotheses.

## 3. Route Shortlist
Show Chennai–Port Blair, Kochi–Lakshadweep and Mumbai–offshore installations. Include route distance, customer segment and evidence source.

## 4. Current Logistics Options
Summarize ship, air freight and helicopter / other options where relevant. Compare door-to-door time, payload, availability and all-in cost on a like-for-like basis.

## 5. Waterfly Value Proposition
Explain the potential middle ground between slow surface transport and expensive air transport. List the product assumptions that must be confirmed: payload, range, speed, sea-state envelope and operating constraints.

## 6. Unit Economics
Show trip cost, cargo delivered and cost per kg for each mode and route. Clearly distinguish sourced data from scenario assumptions.

## 7. Customer Savings
Show cost and time savings against the customer's actual current baseline. Include a sensitivity case for cargo utilization and Waterfly trip cost.

## 8. Route Attractiveness
Apply the weighted score: urgency 25%, potential savings 25%, demand 20%, route suitability 20%, pilot accessibility 10%. Flag any operational or regulatory blockers separately.

## 9. Pilot Proposal
Define customer, cargo, route, trial scope, baseline, pricing approach and success measures.

## 10. Recommendation & Next Steps
State the route to validate first, key unanswered questions, data needed from customer and Waterfly, and decision gates before a pilot.
