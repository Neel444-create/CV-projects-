# Pilot Proposal – Sea-to-Air

## Purpose
Test whether an autonomous electric sea-cargo service can provide measurable value for one narrowly defined customer use case.

## Proposed pilot structure
- **Customer segment:** Select one validated customer from the route assessment.
- **Route:** One route and one origin/destination pair, subject to operational and regulatory approval.
- **Cargo:** A repeatable, non-hazardous, time-sensitive cargo category within the approved payload and handling envelope.
- **Scope:** A limited number of supervised trial deliveries, with conventional transport retained as the baseline/backup.
- **Duration:** Indicative 30-day planning window; actual duration to be agreed with the customer and operating team.
- **Commercial structure:** Trial pricing agreed after validating cost-to-serve, customer value and liability requirements.

## Baseline to capture
1. Current door-to-door delivery time.
2. Current all-in cost per shipment and per kg.
3. Shipment frequency and typical cargo weight.
4. Delay impact or service-level requirement.
5. Current failure, damage or missed-delivery rate, if available.

## Pilot success measures
- Successful delivery rate.
- On-time delivery against the agreed service window.
- All-in cost per kg versus the baseline.
- End-to-end time versus the baseline.
- Payload utilization.
- Customer satisfaction and willingness to continue.

## Go / no-go review
Proceed to a paid or expanded pilot only if:
- Technical and regulatory approvals are in place.
- The route is within the verified operating envelope.
- The customer confirms a recurring use case.
- Safety, insurance, cargo handling and contingency arrangements are agreed.
- Results show customer value without relying on unsupported assumptions.

## Key risks to validate
Weather and sea state; range and payload; launch/recovery access; charging availability; permissions; cargo handling; insurance/liability; schedule reliability; customer procurement cycle.

> This is a proposed planning template, not an operational instruction or a claim that a specific route is currently approved or serviceable.
