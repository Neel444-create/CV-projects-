# Route Research Framework

## Objective
Collect enough evidence to decide whether each route merits a customer conversation and a small pilot—not a full logistics operations study.

## Capture per route
| Field | What to collect |
|---|---|
| Route | Origin, destination and practical endpoints |
| Distance | Approximate route distance; record measurement method |
| Cargo profile | Shipment types and kg per consignment |
| Demand | Indicative shipments per week/month |
| Urgency | Impact of a 1–3 day delay |
| Current mode | Ship, scheduled air, charter, support vessel or helicopter |
| Current cost | Comparable cost for similar shipment size |
| Lead time | Door-to-door delivery time where possible |
| Constraints | Weather, permissions, payload and handling |

## Route hypotheses
### Chennai–Port Blair
Potential use cases: urgent spares, medical supplies and compact high-value cargo. Validate fragmented demand and compare actual sea schedules with available air cargo—not passenger flight time alone.

### Kochi–Lakshadweep
Potential use cases: essential supplies, urgent maintenance spares and administrative cargo. Validate island-specific delivery constraints, frequency and willingness to pay. Identify the exact island endpoint.

### Mumbai–offshore installations
Potential use cases: urgent tools, compact spares and maintenance-critical items. Identify one representative offshore destination and compare the actual incumbent option, including support vessels or helicopter charter.

## Source quality rules
1. Prefer official statistics, operator tariffs and direct quotations.
2. Distinguish route distance from straight-line distance.
3. Record whether cost is per kg, shipment, trip or charter.
4. Normalize comparisons to the same shipment weight and service level.
5. Label each input Verified, Estimated or Illustrative.
6. Never present assumed cargo volume or cost as a market fact.

## Customer interview prompts
- What cargo is most time-sensitive?
- How many urgent consignments do you send monthly?
- What is the usual shipment weight and value?
- Which mode do you use and what does it cost?
- What is the impact of a one-day delay?
- Would a 300 kg faster sea-based option solve a real problem?
- What evidence is needed before a paid pilot?
