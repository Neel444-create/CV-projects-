# Pilot Proposal — Discussion Draft

**Status:** Illustrative proposal for customer discovery, not an approved Waterfly operating plan.

## Objective
Test whether a fast autonomous sea-cargo service can deliver measurable time or cost value for one customer on one route.

## Route selection criteria
- Identified customer and receiving endpoint.
- Recurring urgent cargo, not speculative market volume.
- Shipment weights within the confirmed payload limit.
- Credible incumbent cost and delivery-time baseline.
- Customer willing to share baseline data and feedback.

## Proposed scope
- One customer and one route.
- Small set of pre-agreed cargo categories.
- Payload within confirmed vehicle limit (300 kg is a case-study reference assumption).
- Illustrative pilot window: 30 days, subject to agreement.
- Per-trip or pilot-bundle quote finalized only after actual cost validation.

## Success measures
| Measure | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Delivery reliability | Successful deliveries / attempts |
| Time improvement | Baseline door-to-door time minus pilot time |
| Cost/kg | Comparable trip cost / delivered kg |
| Payload utilization | Delivered kg / available payload kg |
| Customer value | Customer-confirmed value of time saved or disruption avoided |
| Repeat intent | Willingness to continue or expand |

## Go / no-go
Proceed only after route permissions, vehicle range/payload limits, customer economics and measurement approach are validated by responsible parties.

## Commercial next step
Conduct a discovery meeting, obtain a representative shipment profile and incumbent quote, then prepare a route-specific pilot offer. Do not quote from illustrative spreadsheet assumptions.
