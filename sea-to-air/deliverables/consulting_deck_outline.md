# Consulting Deck Outline — Sea-to-Air

1. **Title:** Commercial Feasibility & Unit Economics of Autonomous Sea Cargo.
2. **Executive summary:** urgent small-cargo niche; Mumbai offshore is first discovery hypothesis; economics not yet proven.
3. **Three route hypotheses:** Chennai–Port Blair, Kochi–Lakshadweep, Mumbai offshore; public evidence and key unknowns.
4. **Customer problem:** time-critical compact spares and essential cargo; identify delay cost and shipment frequency.
5. **Incumbent alternatives:** scheduled air cargo, passenger/cargo ships, OSV/PSV/crew boats, helicopters.
6. **Chennai–Port Blair:** 3–4 sailings/month; ~50–60h fair-weather voyage; vehicle range is the gate [S1].
7. **Kochi–Lakshadweep:** 14–18h ship passage; Agatti flight ~1.5h; dedicated cargo vessels exist [S2–S4].
8. **Mumbai offshore:** Mumbai High ~160–165km offshore; OSV/PSV and helicopter alternatives; urgent spares niche [S5–S9].
9. **Unit economics and route screen:** cost/kg, savings, time saved, weighted criteria; blank commercial inputs until verified.
10. **Pilot and next steps:** one operator, one approved installation, one cargo category; baseline, KPIs, go/no-go gates.
